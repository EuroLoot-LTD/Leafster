# Viewing and sign-in access policy

Visitors can browse and search Leafster’s public videos, use Trending and Fresh Finds, open creators’ video profiles, and watch public videos without signing in. Public video and thumbnail URLs support anonymous GET and HEAD requests. Unlisted videos are excluded from discovery but remain accessible through a direct link. Private videos remain restricted to their owner; removed content and suspended creators remain unavailable. All of these checks also apply to the underlying media URLs.

Most interactive and personal features require an approved, active account with its adult staging profile completed: publishing uploads, reacting, subscribing, commenting, reading or publishing Leaves, opening Stories, using collections, history, Studio, settings, notifications and account tools. Moderator functions require an additional server-verified role. Public channel responses omit Leaves for visitors and incomplete profiles. Anonymous requests for personal subscription or liked-video feeds return empty results rather than another account’s information.

The public video exception applies only to GET and HEAD on a narrow, anchored list of catalogue, video-detail, video-media and channel paths. It does not allow mutations. Signed-out requests to protected features return401, and incomplete profiles receive403 until onboarding is complete. Signed-in accounts awaiting onboarding can browse the same public videos as visitors. Signing out removes interaction and personal-data access while preserving anonymous viewing.

Visitors can also open privacy information, terms and community guidelines; check service health; start official Google sign-in; and submit a confidential copyright, illegal-content or child-safety notice. Notice submission uses an anonymous CSRF session and rate limits and does not reveal the moderation queue. Public registration remains closed. Google identity access requires the configured adult staging allowlist, with adult self-declaration during onboarding. Neither Google Sign-In nor that declaration independently verifies age.

Automated tests cover anonymous catalogue/search/discovery, public playback and byte ranges, thumbnails, HEAD requests, direct unlisted links, private/removed/suspended content, channel Leaves filtering, protected community tools, authenticated publishing, incomplete onboarding, sign-out and non-account-holder legal notices. URL case variants and trailing slashes are included. The full media integration test separately verifies anonymous range playback of an actual FFmpeg-processed H.264 video.
